How this works
Everything is stored in this browser under tripOrganizer.v1. That survives a reload and works from a file:// page, but it is per-browser-profile and is wiped by "clear browsing data" — so Export is the durable copy, not a nicety.
Travel times are modelled, not routed. There is no directions API behind this page. Each leg is great-circle distance × a per-mode detour factor ÷ a per-mode speed, plus a fixed wait/boarding overhead; fares are base + per km. Every coefficient is editable per city under Travel → Estimator settings. Expect the right ballpark for planning, and confirm anything time-critical with RATP / Stan / TMB or a live journey planner.
Seeded landmark coordinates are approximate centroids for well-known sites, good to roughly a block. Your hotel, restaurants and bookings are yours to add — click the map, or search an address.
If the map fills with "Access blocked" or "API key required" tiles, change the Basemap dropdown under the map. Tile hosts refuse traffic for their own reasons and some do it with a valid image rather than an error, so the page checks for that and tells you. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors; tiles as credited in the map's corner; address search by Nominatim. Tiles and search need network access — the rest of the page, including every distance, time and schedule, works offline.
